Questions

If your answer isn't here, hit up support.

How do I get access after paying?

The second Stripe confirms your payment, you're auto-redirected to the success page with your license key and access link. A copy is also emailed to whatever address you enter at checkout.

What payment methods do you take?

All processing runs through Stripe: major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Link. We never see or store your card details.

Can I upgrade my tier later?

Yes. Any tier can be topped up to a higher one by purchasing the difference — email support with your license key to arrange it.

What happens when my subscription ends?

Week and Month tiers expire on their exact duration from purchase. Access is revoked at the end of the period. Lifetime tier does not expire.

Do you offer refunds?

Because access is delivered digitally and immediately, we don't offer refunds after redemption. If your license key was never redeemed and something went wrong on our end, contact support.

How do I recover my license?

Go to My Orders and enter the email you used at checkout. All your keys and access links will be shown.
